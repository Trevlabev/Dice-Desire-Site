01 / THE CONCEPT

The game is not a prop.

Dice & Desire is designed to work as entertainment in its own right: recurring characters, consequences, comedy, combat, relationships, and story first. The adult dimension adds another creative layer without replacing the campaign.

01

Genuine campaign

A real ongoing fantasy story with consequences, choices, progression, and an ensemble viewers can invest in.

02

Adult by consent

Flirting, roleplayed sexuality, nudity, or bonus content can vary by performer. No universal explicit-content requirement.

03

Serialized story

Sessions become narrative chapters so viewers have a reason to follow the cast, story, and relationships over time.

04

Creator-led

The shared project should amplify participating creators, not flatten them into a single anonymous brand.

08 / FAQ

The short version.

Is this actually a tabletop campaign?

Yes. The game is not a prop. Story, characters, improvisation, consequences, combat, exploration, and cast chemistry are the foundation of the project.

Is explicit content required?

No. Adult participation is performer-specific, and different people can have different content ceilings without weakening the ensemble.

Is the game system finalized?

Not publicly locked yet. The current direction favors an original setting and original story while the exact tabletop rules approach is still being finalized.

How long are episodes?

The current working target is roughly 25–40 minutes, with episodes ending on natural narrative beats rather than arbitrary timestamps.

How often would episodes release?

Twice weekly is the current experiment being considered. Pilot Zero will determine whether that cadence is actually sustainable.

Is casting open?

The project is still in development and the interest process is being designed. Recruitment will prioritize reliability, chemistry, communication, roleplay, boundary clarity, and creator professionalism.

Is Pilot Zero public?

No. It is private by default and exists to test chemistry, comfort, production quality, pacing, and editing workload before any launch decision.

01 / THE SHOW

The fantasy is better when the game is real.

Dice & Desire is a serialized adult fantasy actual-play project. The campaign is not a prop: choices, dice, recurring characters, relationships, failures, combat, and cast dynamics carry the show.

The “after dark” layer can be flirtation, roleplayed sexuality, suggestive presentation, nudity, or separate bonus material — according to each performer’s boundaries.

02 / THE FORMAT

One night at the table.
Weeks of story.

The leading model separates a recording session from a released episode. We keep the tabletop rhythm natural, then shape the best material into chapters people can actually follow.

~4 hrRECORD

A normal tabletop session with room for roleplay, story beats and combat.

~2 hrEDITED

Release-quality story after dead air, setup, delays and weaker beats are trimmed.

~4EPISODES

Roughly 25–40 minutes each, cut around story beats rather than timestamps.

~2 weeksRELEASES

At two episodes per week, one strong session can create a meaningful buffer.

Working example only. Pilot Zero determines the actual editing yield, episode length and sustainable cadence.

SERIALIZATION

A chapter should feel like an episode — not a chopped Zoom call.

Natural cliffhangers, reveals, wins, losses and scene changes decide the cut. Runtime is a target, not the editor.

06 / FAQ

The short version.

The project is still in development. Pilot Zero exists specifically so we can learn what is actually sustainable before pretending everything is finalized.

Is this really a tabletop campaign?

Yes. The game is the backbone of the project, not set dressing.

Is explicit content required?

No. Different performers can participate at completely different adult-content levels.

How long are episodes?

The working target is roughly 25–40 minutes, cut at natural story beats rather than arbitrary timestamps.

How often would episodes release?

Twice weekly is the leading experiment, subject to what Pilot Zero teaches us.

Is Pilot Zero public?

No. Private by default.

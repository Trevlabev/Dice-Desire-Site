01 / The concept

The game is not a prop.

Dice & Desire only works if the campaign is worth following on its own. The adult tone adds another layer; it does not replace characters, choices, danger, comedy, or genuine play.

REAL CAMPAIGN

Consequences matter.

Original story, recurring relationships, exploration, combat, and choices that carry forward.

ADULT BY CONSENT

Different ceilings can coexist.

Adult participation is granular and performer-specific rather than a uniform expectation.

SERIALIZED

Give viewers a reason to return.

Episodes are chapters in an unfolding story instead of isolated clips wearing a campaign costume.

CREATOR-LED

The ensemble stays visible.

The show should deepen attachment to the cast while supporting each creator’s individual identity.

02 / Working format

One table session.

05 / FAQ

The short version.

Is this actually a tabletop campaign?

Yes. The game is the foundation rather than a decorative excuse for unrelated content.

Is explicit content required?

No. Adult participation is granular and performer-specific. Different cast members can have different boundaries.

How long are episodes?

The current experiment is roughly 25–40 minutes per released chapter, with cuts based on natural story beats rather than an arbitrary timer.

How often could episodes release?

Twice weekly is a working experiment. Pilot Zero will determine whether that cadence is genuinely sustainable.

Is casting open?

The project is still in development and the recruitment process is being structured. Initial selection emphasizes reliability, chemistry, roleplay, communication, and boundary literacy.

Is Pilot Zero public?

No. It is private by default and exists to test chemistry, comfort, technology, pacing, and editing before public production.

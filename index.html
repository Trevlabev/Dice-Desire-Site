01 / The concept

The game is not a prop.

Dice & Desire works only if the campaign is worth following on its own. Adult tone adds another layer; it does not replace characters, choices, danger, comedy, or actual play.

REAL CAMPAIGN

Consequences matter.

Original story, recurring relationships, exploration, combat and choices that carry forward.

ADULT BY CONSENT

Different ceilings can coexist.

Adult participation is granular and performer-specific rather than a uniform requirement.

SERIALIZED

Give viewers a reason to return.

Episodes are chapters in an unfolding story, not isolated clips pretending to be a campaign.

CREATOR-LED

The ensemble stays visible.

The project should build attachment to the cast and support each creator’s individual identity.

07 / FAQ

The short version.

The project is still in development. Pilot Zero exists specifically so the format can be tested before pretending every detail is finalized.

Is this actually a tabletop campaign?

Yes. The game is the backbone of the project, not set dressing.

Is explicit content required?

No. Different performers can participate at completely different adult-content levels.

How long are episodes?

The working target is roughly 25–40 minutes, cut at natural story beats rather than arbitrary timestamps.

How often would episodes release?

Twice weekly is the leading experiment, subject to what Pilot Zero teaches us about editing yield and sustainability.

What is Pilot Zero?

A private proof-of-concept used to test cast chemistry, comfort, technical quality, GM pacing, editing load and promotional clip potential before public release.

Is casting open?

The project is in active development and the interest process is being designed. Current selection priorities are reliability, ensemble chemistry, improvisation, boundary clarity and creator professionalism.
